The five levels

FIT’s Generative AI Skills levels. A resource’s level is the point it takes you to in that skill area, judged from what the course actually asks you to do — not from its title. Level 0 means no exposure and is never assigned to a resource.

Level 1AwareCan describe AI in plain terms, spot where it is being used, name obvious risks
Level 2Assisted userCan use a mainstream AI tool for a defined task when given a template or walkthrough
Level 3Independent userChooses the tool unprompted, works without a script, appraises output critically
Level 4PractitionerBuilds, configures, integrates or teaches; evaluates rigorously; handles failure modes
Level 5Specialist / leadSets standards, governs, designs novel solutions, owns outcomes at organisational scale
